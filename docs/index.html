VARNA

A free, fully offline voice assistant for Windows.
Control your PC with natural speech — no internet, no cloud, no data collection.

⬇ Download for Windows
⭐ View Source on GitHub

What Can VARNA Do?

📦

Open & Close Any App

"open whatsapp", "close spotify" — scans your entire PC, handles typos like "watsapp" automatically.

🪟

Window Intelligence

"switch to chrome", "minimize vscode", "maximize edge" — smart restore, focus, and launch.

⌨️

Voice Typing

"type hello world" — types in any active app. 40+ key commands: undo, copy, paste, enter.

🔍

Smart Search

"search React hooks" — searches in active browser or opens new tab. YouTube search built-in.

🧠

Natural Language

"can you help me open notepad" → just works. 60+ filler phrases understood. Handles accents.

🔒

100% Offline & Secure

Voice never leaves your PC. Whitelist-only execution. 4-gate safety architecture.

⚡

Custom Macros

"whenever I say focus mode, open vscode and open chrome" — chain multiple commands.

🗂️

Drive & Folder Navigation

"go to D drive", "go to downloads", "go back" — navigate your entire filesystem.

📸

Screenshots & Clipboard

"screenshot as Bug1", "read clipboard", "paste 3rd item" — all managed by voice.

How It Works

🎤 Microphone → 📝 Whisper STT (offline) → 🧹 NLP Pipeline (4 layers) → 🧠 Parser → 🛡️ Safety Check → ⚡ Execute → 🔊 Voice Response
